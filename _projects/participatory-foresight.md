<div class="proj-section">
<h2>Research objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Anticipating the consequences of deployment.</b> Surface the ethical, social and practical consequences of speech-based Parkinson’s detection while the technology can still be shaped.</li>
  <li><b>Examining competing stakeholder values.</b> Identify areas of consensus and divergence among patients, caregivers, clinicians and ethicists.</li>
  <li><b>Establishing conditions for responsible deployment.</b> Translate these perspectives into a governance framework of conditions for using speech biomarkers in Parkinson’s care.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="proj-section">
<h2>Participatory approach (planned)</h2>
<p><b>Six deployment scenarios.</b> Plausible future deployment scenarios, each tied to evidence from the HEARD experiments:</p>
<ol>
  <li>A speech score during a clinic visit</li>
  <li>Weekly speech recordings at home</li>
  <li>When the tool and the clinician disagree</li>
  <li>Speech changes before a diagnosis</li>
  <li>A tool that works better for some people than others</li>
  <li>What happens to voice recordings</li>
</ol>
<p><b>Stakeholder representation.</b> Speech-language pathologists, neurologists, responsible-AI researchers and ethicists, people with Parkinson’s disease, and caregivers.</p>
<p><b>Data collection and analysis.</b> A survey and follow-up interviews, analysed for where stakeholders agree and disagree and what they require before deployment.</p>
<p><b>Empirical foundations.</b> Results from Papers 2 and 3 of HEARD: how models perform at new sites, reliability of model calibration, how much speech each person needs, and what models rely on besides the disease.</p>
</div>

<div class="proj-section">
<h2>Research status</h2>
<p>Study design. The six deployment scenarios are designed, the survey and interview script are in preparation, and recruitment will begin after ethics approval of the study amendment. No findings are reported yet.</p>
